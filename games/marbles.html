How to play Ringer
13 marbles sit in a cross in the ring. Tap a spot on the ring’s edge to place your shooter, then pull back and let go to shoot.
Knock a marble out of the ring to keep it. If you knock one out and your shooter stays inside, shoot again from where it stopped.
Whoever wins the most marbles wins (7 wins it).
