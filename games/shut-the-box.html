Shut the Box

Scores
How to play
Roll the dice, then flip down any open tiles that add up to your roll (a 9 could be 9, 4+5, or 1+3+5). Keep rolling until you can’t make the number. Your score is the tiles left standing, so low is good. Flip every tile and you Shut the Box for a perfect 0! Once 7, 8 and 9 are down, you may roll just one die.

Shut the Box

Roll the dice, flip the tiles, and try to shut every one. Lowest score wins.
