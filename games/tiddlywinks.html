Tiddlywinks

POT
Winks in the pot
How to play
Tap one of your winks, then pull back from it and let go, like a slingshot. Farther pull = bigger flick, and the line turns red at full power.
Winks wobble in the air, so long shots are hard. Hit the rim and you bounce off. Too short a hop can’t clear the cup’s wall.
Pot a wink to score and flick again.
Land on someone’s wink to cover it (a “squop”). Covered winks can’t move until uncovered.
First to pot all their winks wins, or most in the pot after 15 rounds.
