Color Crash

Match the color or the number, play your power cards, and be the first to empty your hand. Down to one card? Hit CRASH! before someone catches you.

Pick a color

How to play

Goal

Be the first to play every card in your hand.

Your turn

Play one card that matches the top card’s color or its number or symbol. A Wild can go on anything. Glowing cards are the ones you can play. Can’t play? Tap the draw pile. If the card you draw fits, you can play it right away, or keep it and pass.

Power cards

⦸ Skip: the next player loses their turn.
⇄ Reverse: play switches direction. With two players it works like a Skip.
+2: the next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
Wild: you choose the new color.
Wild +4: you choose the color, and the next player draws 4 and loses their turn.

CRASH!

When you play your second-to-last card, hit CRASH! before the next player goes. If someone catches you first, you draw 2.

Scoring

The round winner scores the cards left in everyone else’s hand: numbers count their number, Skip, Reverse and +2 are 20, and Wilds are 50. Play one round, or race to 250 points.
